- **Lions:** Asked for 20 lions with the moving skill that wander every step, drawn as orange circles of size 3, shown in one GUI display. Implemented a commented lion species with a wander reflex and an orange `circle(3)` aspect, created 20 of them in the global init, and added a GUI experiment with one display of the lions.
  Tested in GAMA: ran correctly on the first try; nothing rejected or corrected.
- **Zebras:** Asked for 20 zebras with the moving skill that wander every step, drawn as grey triangles of size 2, added to the display, and nothing else. Implemented a commented zebra species with a wander reflex and a grey `triangle(2)` aspect, created 20 of them in the global init, and drew them in the display under the lions.
  Tested in GAMA: ran correctly on the first try; nothing rejected or corrected.
- **Hunting:** Asked for a lion reflex that, every step, makes every zebra within distance 3 (`at_distance`) execute `die`. Implemented a commented `hunt` reflex on the lion, after its walk, that asks every zebra at distance 3 to die.
  Tested in GAMA: ran correctly on the first try; nothing rejected or corrected.
- **Grass grid:** Asked for the 50 x 50 grassland grid from the spec, between the species and the experiment, with biomass starting at `rnd(10.0)`, growing 0.1 per step, and coloured a shade of green that follows biomass and is recalculated every step, drawn under the animals. Implemented a commented `grass` grid whose biomass and colour both use `update:` (pale green at 0 kg to dark green at 10 kg or more), drawn as the first layer of the display.
  Tested in GAMA: ran correctly on the first try; nothing rejected or corrected.
- **Zebras eat grass (Extension 1):** Asked, spec first, for zebras to eat grass: every step a zebra reduces the biomass of the cell it stands on by 1 kg, never below 0. Implemented spec section 8 and a commented `eat` reflex on the zebra, after its walk, that takes 1 kg from the cell under it (`grass(location)`) without going below 0.
  Tested in GAMA: eating worked (a temporary console check showed total grass growing about 4,700 kg per 20 cycles instead of 5,000), but it was invisible because biomass had no cap and every cell above 10 kg looked the same; a 0–30 kg colour scale was tried and rejected, and instead biomass was capped at 10 kg per cell with the 0–10 kg colour scale kept, which makes the grazing trails visible.
- **Lion energy (Extension 2):** Asked, spec first, for a lion `energy` attribute starting at 10, costing 0.5 per step of movement, gaining 5 per zebra killed, with death at 0 or below, plus monitors for the number of lions and zebras. Implemented spec section 9, a commented `energy` attribute, the cost in the walk reflex, +5 per kill in the hunt reflex, a `starve` reflex checked at the end of each step, and two monitors.
  Tested in GAMA: no lions were visible and zebras stopped being killed, because the lions starved (most died at step 19, all by about step 30–40); corrected the moving cost to 0.1 per step, so lions now last until about step 200–250, and rounded energy to one decimal so a lion dies exactly when it reaches 0. The monitors were later replaced by a temporary console check, which was then removed.
